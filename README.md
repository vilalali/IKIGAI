## 📖 About This Project

**Ikigai** (生き甲斐) is a Japanese concept referring to something that gives a person a sense of purpose, a reason for living. This repository serves as a centralized hub for my intellectual journey—a personal archive of literature, studies, and notes spanning various domains without limitations. 

The goal is to explore everything from economics and markets to philosophy, science, and the arts, fostering a broad and interconnected understanding of the world.
